I'm Marius Gjerd – a developer based in Bergen, Norway, working at the crossroads of code and physical infrastructure.

I started out as an electrician. After getting my trade certificate, I spent time on actual industrial installations before transitioning into software. For the past few years I've been building IoT solutions in the energy sector at Prelect, where the code I write talks to physical systems out in the real world.

This autumn I'm starting a three-year degree in Industrial Automation at Fagskulen Vestland, alongside my day job. The degree covers PLCs, instrumentation, industrial networking, and OT security – the formal industrial counterpart to the IT side I already know.

**What I'm interested in**

The bridge between IT and OT. The cloud-connected world of developers and the physical world of industrial control systems don't speak the same language, even when they have to work together.

**What I write about here**

What I learn along the way – technical deep-dives when something deserves it, shorter reflections when something doesn't. The code behind the projects lives in the open on [GitHub](https://github.com/MariusGjerd).
